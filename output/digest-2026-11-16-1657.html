Its language backbone inherits the hybrid design of MiMo-V2-Flash, which mixes sliding-window attention (SWA) with global self-attention. NaiveAI's recipe starts from those weights. Through continued, multi-stage training, the team adapted the checkpoint to DeepSeek Sparse Attention layers. According to the NaiveAI technical blog , this took roughly 3.25T additional tokens on top of MiMo's original 48T.
