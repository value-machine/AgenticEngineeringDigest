In his AIE presentation, Singh noted that agent tasks at Arca can take up to ten days. “It's very stop-start, there's many actors in between, many decision points, many handoffs,” he said. For this reason, Arca has developed an internal memory system . “So we have this persistence for every client ,” Singh said in our podcast discussion. “What has happened so far? What are we tracking for next? What needs to happen immediately in the next couple of days [and] what are we tracking for the long term? Which individual is responsible for which action? All of this is being tracked by our memory systems that our agents contribute to.” Arca uses models from frontier labs like Anthropic and OpenAI, with Claude its primary model provider.
